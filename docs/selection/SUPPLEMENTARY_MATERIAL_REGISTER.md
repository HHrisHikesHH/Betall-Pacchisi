# Supplementary Material Register

> **Selection dossier.** A register of material **outside the 27 candidate families**, kept so that the wider tradition stays visible without delaying the choice of the 25. **Nothing here is a candidate, and nothing here is a supplementary tale.** An item could become a candidate only through the outside-candidate gate below and the owner's own promotion decision. Compiled 2026-11-18 by an AI session (Claude Code); not independently reviewed. No new research was done for this register: it consolidates what the repository already records.

| **B. No outside lead has been established as a distinct Betal-narrated tale. |
| **C. Related material and research leads** | Parallels, embedded stories, uncertain attributions, unexamined recensions and unverified leads | This register |

Guiding principle: **complete the 25 first; preserve the wider tradition without letting it delay the 25.**

## The outside-candidate gate

An outside lead may be **investigated** only when there is a credible reason to think it could:

1. be a genuine, distinct tale **narrated by Betal** (D-035);
2. fall within scope: associated with Vikram–Betal (D-001), not another folk tradition (D-002); a wholly invented tale would need the owner's explicit decision (Q-411);
3. materially improve the collection or fill a genuine gap.

Anything that passes a bounded investigation goes to the owner for a **promotion decision**. It is not added to the 27 or the 25 by anyone else. Being mentioned in a scholarly source does not make a lead a candidate.

## Status labels used here

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified scholarly lead** | A named scholar's statement that the material exists, read in the repository's research; its content is not known |
| **Primary-text contents only** | A table of contents or heading was read; the text itself was not |
| **Partial or indirect evidence** | Some content is known, through a translation or report, but not enough to assess it as a story |
| **Established parallel to an existing candidate** | The material has been read and is a version of one of the 27 |
| **Unverified** | Mentioned or found as a search lead; not confirmed |
| **Not accessible** | Located but could not be opened |
| **Adaptation, not a source tradition** | A later literary reworking with its own inventions |
