# Selection Dossier: the 27 Candidate Families

This folder helps the owner choose **the 25 distinct Betal-narrated tales** that form the core collection (D-035, Q-411). It turns the existing research into one place where each candidate's story, dilemma, versions and overlaps can be understood without reading the large research reports.

**It does not choose the 25, rank the candidates, draft any story or settle any open question.** Every decision listed below stays with the owner (D-022).

Compiled 2026-11-18 by an AI session (Claude Code) on branch `selection/27-candidate-assessment`. Not independently reviewed: a separate review is planned, and this session's own checks are not a review (D-020).

## The frozen scope: 27 candidates

- **TC-001 to TC-024:** the 24 core families shared by Somadeva, Śivadāsa, the Hindi tradition and (mostly) Jambhaladatta.
- **TC-025 to TC-027:** the three families found only in Jambhaladatta (J 21–23).
- **TC-023 is one family and one slot** with two recorded Hindi variants (XXII and XXIV), by the owner's earlier decision. It is not reopened.
- The outer Vikram–Betal frame is **not** a tale and is not counted (D-035).
- The pool is not expanded here. An outside lead can become a candidate only by the owner's promotion decision (see the register's gate).

## Reading a story bucket

Every bucket has the same eight sections: (1) identity and summary; (2) the central dilemma; (3) a version-and-evidence table; (4) the common story spine and the variant differences; (5) storytelling potential, entertainment first (D-036); (6) contribution to the collection; (7) editorial and sensitivity considerations; (8) a provisional assessment with reasons and confidence.

**Evidence tags** mark how each claim is known:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Primary] | A passage read in a primary text (KSS e-text; Ś in Uhle via OCR; the Hindi on Barker page images; J's Sanskrit in Emeneau). Passage-level, by an AI reader |
| [Translation] | Read in a scholarly translation or edition (Platts; Emeneau; Jørgensen's German; Tawney/Penzer) |
| [Secondary] | A scholar's report about a text |
| [Provisional] | A catalogue-level or concordance correspondence not checked tale by tale (e.g. Kṣemendra) |
| [Inference] | The analyst's interpretation, especially in sections 5 and 6 |
| [Unknown] | Not established |

**Locators:** `KSS 12.17.62` (book.taraṅga.verse); `N1 TC-010` ([SOMADEVA_TALE_EVIDENCE.md](../research/SOMADEVA_TALE_EVIDENCE.md)); `N2` ([HINDI_XXII_XXIV_COMPARISON.md](../research/HINDI_XXII_XXIV_COMPARISON.md)); `N3 §n` ([JAMBHALADATTA_ADDITIONAL_TALES_EVIDENCE.md](../research/JAMBHALADATTA_ADDITIONAL_TALES_EVIDENCE.md)); `Cat.` ([CANDIDATE_TALE_CATALOGUE.md](../research/CANDIDATE_TALE_CATALOGUE.md)); `Report` ([SOURCE_TRADITIONS_COMPARISON.md](../research/SOURCE_TRADITIONS_COMPARISON.md)); `T p.` / `Tr p.` for Emeneau's text and translation pages.

**Provisional labels** (recommendations for discussion, not decisions):

- **Strong core contender:** a distinct dilemma, good evidence, a strong story engine, no serious overlap.
- **Viable core contender:** workable and distinct, with a real hesitation (handling difficulty, thinner evidence, a repeated form).
- **Distinctness or overlap contested:** a serious question whether it competes for the same slot as another candidate.
- **Evidence insufficient to judge:** too little evidence to assess (no candidate received this label).

The labels are not a ranking.

## Reading the comparison buckets

Each group tests a suspected overlap against the actual events and dilemmas, and ends with one of four conclusions: *same underlying story*; *related stories, distinct central dilemmas*; *shared motif or device only*; *insufficient evidence*. A conclusion that two tales are distinct is not advice to include both.

- Whether TC-024 is one of the 25, part of the frame, or both (Q-002).

- Whether any research item (N4–N9) is commissioned, and whether any outside lead is investigated or promoted.

## Evidence limitations

- **All readings are AI readings.** No Sanskritist, Hindi/Braj or Newari specialist has checked them.
- **KSS** was read in full (N1), in an e-text not collated with the 1915 print.
- **Śivadāsa** was read through diacritic-less OCR, mainly at questions and answers.
- **The Hindi** was read mainly through Platts's English; a few passages on Barker's page images.
- **Jambhaladatta**: J 10, J 13, J 21–23 and J 25 are read; the rest are not. J 22 and J 23 rest on one Bengali manuscript plus a poor print.
- **Kṣemendra** is a provisional concordance; only two tales were checked.
- **Entertainment and emotional assessments** are inferences from recorded events.
- The story buckets run longer than the 600–1,000 words planned, because of their evidence tables.
