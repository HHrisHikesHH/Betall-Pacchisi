## 4. Blueprint (A2): what it must contain, and what is already fixed

The A2 blueprint follows §9.1 (chronology, motivations, essential events, source Q/A **verbatim**, adapted Q/A in D-041 form, role table, safeguards, open questions, confidence).

**Fixed by the slate audit and D-048, not to be reopened here:**
- The answer and Betal's objection stay **at full strength**; the dilemma stays hard.
- The "love's arrows" and "served his master" exemptions are **dropped**: the adviser and the prince did their own wrongs; the *deaths* belong to the one with the duty to inquire.
- The branding is told as a **violation**, with the prince's hand visible; it is not told as cleverness.
- Padmāvatī keeps her origin as an ivory-carver's daughter; she gets a moment of **understanding**, not an invented choice, after the banishment; the source does not record her consent to leaving and that silence stays felt.
- The ambiguity over whether she poisoned anyone **stays**, so the same moral standard applies to her.
- Must keep: at least one decoded sign, the false story told to authority, the king's verdict, the parents' deaths, the objection. May alter: the number of signs, the dog, the Benares opening. Set up the parents early.
