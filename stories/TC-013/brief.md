## 4. Blueprint (A2): what it must contain, and what is already fixed by the audit

The A2 blueprint follows PRODUCTION_PLAN §9.1: chronology, motivations, essential events, the source question and answer **verbatim**, the adapted question and answer (D-041 form), the role table (charter §3.6), safeguards, open questions and confidence.

**Fixed by the slate audit (D-038 to D-048), not to be reopened here:**
- The answer stays: no actor is guilty; the sin falls on whoever blames without reflection. The dilemma is untouched.
- The *brahmahatyā* worth-weighting is removed from the narrated question (charter §3.2.5b): it asks about the guilt of this man's death. His caste identity and his host's are kept (a sympathetic victim and a kind household).
- The host's wife becomes a named, interior person. Her expulsion by her husband is one instance the reader may connect to the verdict; **the husband is not made "the" judge** (that would narrow the source).
- The dying man's accusation is rendered as a dying man's belief, without the caste weighting.
- The abducted wife is felt as a loss. **Do not invent a rescue.**
