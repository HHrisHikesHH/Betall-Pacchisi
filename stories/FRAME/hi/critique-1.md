## 6. Story within story and the combined experience

- **Who speaks:** clear. Betal says «राजा»; Vikram answers by name. «तपस्वी» is correctly absent from the frame.
- **Penalty position:** correct. T59 question, B1 penalty, B2 hesitation, T60 answer, T61 objection, T62–T66 reply (D-057B). B1's «जैसे कोई छोटी-सी बात याद आ गई हो» is a fine comic placement. The penalty is not restated at T61, which is right.
- **Echo (FR-14, MODERATE):** A21–A22 puts a patient man drawing circles in a cremation ground, wanting a corpse; minutes later T41–T50 gives a **false** तपस्वी in a cremation ground whose «घेरा» story deceives a king who obeys him. T65 then has Vikram fault a king for relying on one man he did not test, after Vikram's own «मंज़ूर है». The irony is rich. It also reads as a hint against क्षान्तिशील, which D-058 item 1 and §5.6 were meant to avoid in frame text, and it doubles the stock deceiving-ascetic picture (charter §3.2.8; TC-001 A3 finding 14). Neither text alone is at fault. **User decision:** accept the echo knowingly (it comes from the source order and the chosen first tale; this critic leans to accept, with no new frame wording that strengthens it), or soften «घेरा» or the frame's imagery. A sensitivity reader should see the pair.

## 8. Ethics and character

Passes on this AI reading, subject to FR-07 and FR-14. The treasurer is a person (A7–A10). The king is judged by events: he snaps (A39) and grips his sword without knowing it (A42); nothing praises him. Betal is witty and coercive, no moral authority. No caste, sect or backstory marker. No women in the frame, as recorded; in the combined reading Padmāvatī's removal is followed at once by a joke (C2) and the return: the beat is brief and mocks Vikram, not her, so no finding, but a human reader should check the tonal turn. The macabre is restrained (D-068).
