## Verdict

The core is strong and the draft is close: the child in the fire (¶34–38), the waking told from her side (¶55–60), and "तुमने दुख नहीं पूछा" give it grip. The D-051 handling is sound. The wish is labelled in the narrator's voice, her later words are left unrecorded (¶68), and the verdict is not ratified by her. The weaknesses are a logic hole (the mantra, #1), an unpaid promise (the names, #3), and an over-long, over-asided front half.

The three changes that would most raise grip:
1. Fix the mantra/theft logic (#1) and pay off "नाम" in the quarrel (#3).
2. Cut the front half (¶7, ¶13, ¶18, ¶62 and the asides) and move the "and you too" turn out of the quarrel's peak (#5, #13, #14).
3. Rework the ending (#12): drop the echoes of the frame and TC-001, and add one forward line from Betal.
